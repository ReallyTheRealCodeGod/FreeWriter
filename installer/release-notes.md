FreeWriter is a distraction-free writing app that works like an old
typewriter: set a goal, and the page holds you until you reach it.

### Windows

Download **FreeWriter-Setup.exe** below and open it.

- If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose **Keep**
  (in Edge: the three dots, then **Keep**, then **Keep anyway**).
- Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". Click
  **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It says this because FreeWriter isn't
  signed with a paid certificate, not because anything is wrong.
- Click **Install**, then **Finish**. FreeWriter is in the Start menu.

### Mac (new, not yet tried much)

Download **FreeWriter.dmg** below and open it. Drag **FreeWriter** onto the
**Applications** folder next to it.

The first time you open FreeWriter, macOS stops it, because it isn't signed
with a paid Apple certificate:

1. Close the message (**Done** or **Cancel**, not Move to Trash).
2. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to "FreeWriter was blocked" and click **Open Anyway**, then
   **Open Anyway** again (you may need your Mac password).

After that it opens like any other app. It asks once to use your Documents
folder, where it keeps your pages: click **Allow**.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or macOS 11 or later.
